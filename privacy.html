Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 18, 2026

Overview

Daily NLP is designed to minimize data collection. We do not store your personal reflections or recordings on our own servers.

How your data is processed

When you use reflection features, your content is sent to third-party providers for processing:

  • Groq.com for transcription.
  • OpenAI for AI analysis and related model responses.

What we store

We do not store your reflections, transcripts, or analysis results on our servers.

Third-party processing

Because Groq and OpenAI process your reflections to provide core app functionality, their systems may temporarily process data according to their own terms and privacy policies. Please review those policies directly:

  • Groq Privacy Policy
  • OpenAI Privacy Policy

Children's privacy

Daily NLP is not intended for children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
